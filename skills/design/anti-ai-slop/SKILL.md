# Anti AI Slop

## Use this skill when

Creating, implementing, or reviewing a user interface or visual system.

## Workflow

1. Inspect product, audience, content, existing design language, and constraints first.
2. Choose a coherent direction with restrained palette, intentional typography, spacing rhythm, and hierarchy.
3. Avoid default gradients, neon glow, glass effects, oversized rounded cards, decorative blobs, and repetitive dashboard grids.
4. Use components when they clarify structure; design real loading, empty, error, and success states.
5. Prioritize readable contrast, responsive behavior, keyboard use, and content.

## Verification

Review viewports, hierarchy, contrast, focus, overflow, states, and real interactions.

## Boundaries

Do not redesign the whole product without permission or claim visual verification without viewing a render.
